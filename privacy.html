Privacy Policy — CreatorBot

Last updated: 2026-09-08

CreatorBot is a personal, single-operator tool used to prepare and upload short-form video content to the operator's own social media accounts. It is not a public service and has no end users other than its operator.

Information we access

When you connect a TikTok account, CreatorBot receives and stores:

  • Your TikTok account identifier (open_id) and display name
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok for that account

These are obtained only after you explicitly authorize the application, and only for the scopes shown on the TikTok authorization screen (user.info.basic, video.upload).

How we use it

  • To upload video files you created to your own TikTok account as drafts, which appear in your TikTok inbox for you to review, edit, and publish yourself
  • To display which account is connected, so uploads are not sent to the wrong account
  • To refresh the access token so the connection keeps working

CreatorBot never posts publicly on your behalf. Uploaded videos remain drafts until you publish them from the TikTok app.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored encrypted on a private server controlled by the operator. They are kept only while the account remains connected. Disconnecting the account in CreatorBot deletes the stored tokens immediately, and the authorization can also be revoked at any time from your TikTok account settings.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics. Data is transmitted only between CreatorBot and TikTok's official API endpoints.

Content

Video files uploaded through CreatorBot are created by the operator and are transferred directly to TikTok. CreatorBot does not read, analyze, or retain content from your TikTok account.

Your choices

  • Disconnect the account in CreatorBot at any time to delete stored tokens
  • Revoke access from TikTok's own settings at any time
  • Request deletion of any stored data by contacting the operator
